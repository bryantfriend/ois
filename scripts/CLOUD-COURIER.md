Cloud Courier replaces the old Airplane lane renderer. It preserves the `airplane` format, the four existing lesson IDs, answer pools, teacher editing, saved lesson format, 100-point scoring and three-shield limit. Older teacher lesson titles remain valid; prepared Airplane titles are displayed as Cloud Courier. The library also finds the game by its former Airplane name.

Students read a prompt and choose a labelled village, then deliberately launch a delivery. Planning has no countdown. The aircraft follows the chosen route, drops its parcel and receives illustrated feedback through the shared translucent answer overlay. Wrong addresses cost one shield; three mistakes end the route. At the end an in-scene delivery report offers replay, editing and return to the library. Selection is available by pointer, Up/Down or 1–4; Enter delivers and P pauses. Pause freezes route time and the aircraft. Reduced motion removes ambient effects and shortens travel. The plane retains a constant size across answers.

The new module intercepts only Airplane's render/step/movement paths. It uses existing activity scoring and round advancement. Other formats retain their own renderers and feedback. Narration was not added; the shared sound controls and Sonia runtime stay in place, and pause/navigation/restart cancel pending speech.

Generated assets use the approved first concept in `output/concepts/airplane/01-cloud-courier.png` as their reference. Original PNG masters and the full built-in image-generation prompts are saved in `output/concepts/cloud-courier/`. Runtime WebP files live in `dist/assets/cloud-courier/`; the three gameplay images total 332,192 bytes, and the small preview is separate. `python scripts/build-cloud-courier-art.py` rebuilds them with Pillow; `node scripts/build-loading.cjs` includes only the three gameplay images in the Airplane loading gate. No large PNG masters are shipped in dist.

`qa-cloud-courier.mjs` covers pointer and keyboard delivery, all four original packs, preserved editable content, no answer timeout, constant plane scale, single scoring, success/failure, shields, pause/resume, completion and replay, English/maths/edited science, responsive layouts, reduced motion, escaped question text and navigation isolation. `qa-answer-overlay.mjs` and `qa-library-styles.mjs` also exercise the updated game through shared UI paths.
