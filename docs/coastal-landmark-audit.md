# Coastal Connections landmark audit

Reviewed all 70 destination badges. Replaced 43 illustrations (16 UK-network, 27 Kyrgyzstan); retained 27 distinct existing badges. The UK network includes Ireland and continental European stops in later rounds. Museum badges can represent a characteristic exhibit rather than a building exterior. Nearby destinations are explicitly labelled in the passport.
