# Coastal Connections roadmap

This is a planning document. Future features below are not implemented or scheduled.

## Current review build

The UK campaign introduces England, Wales and Scotland, then Ireland, flights and mainland Europe. Regular trains unlock in round 8; high-speed trains unlock in round 20. The current map includes France, Belgium, the Netherlands and Germany. The Channel Tunnel service unlocks in round 18. Fog, landmarks, capitals, waterways and area discovery facts support geography learning.

Keep improving this campaign before adding more destinations. Existing vehicle upgrades, station construction, passenger queues, round rewards and good/bad card choices form the shared game system.

## 1. Finish and balance the UK campaign

- Playtest the bus-only first seven rounds and tune demand, rewards and upgrade costs so expansion remains achievable.
- Add short introductions when a country or transport unlocks, with a clear explanation of how to use it.
- Improve dense-map readability, station selection and interactive-board touch controls.
- Add a teacher mode: pause demand, adjust difficulty, select a starting round, and disable loss timers for demonstrations.
- Offer a brief end-of-run learning recap with countries, capitals, waterways and landmarks encountered.
- Add accessible colour-independent route patterns, adjustable text size, reduced motion and keyboard support for every game action.
- Version saved games so future world changes have a clear restart or migration path.

## 2. Expand Europe

- Add neighbouring countries in small, researched groups, including their capitals, major waterways and memorable landmarks.
- Plan unlock order around connected land networks, ferry crossings and major mountain barriers.
- Add geography challenges such as crossing the Alps, serving coastal towns and linking inland capitals.
- Expand high-speed rail upgrades and international connections with clear costs and distinct vehicle art.
- Create three sourced discovery facts per new area, and offer optional map questions between rounds.
- Balance route lengths and map scale; add regional views before the whole continent becomes crowded.

## 3. Introduce selectable continents

- Add a campaign selector with separate saved runs for each region.
- Build reusable world data for boundaries, cities, capitals, rivers, landmarks, unlocks and facts.
- Research and playtest each continent independently rather than copying European geography or transport assumptions.
- Give each campaign suitable transport choices, terrain challenges, art and card pools.
- Include free exploration alongside the round-based challenge mode.

## 4. Historical campaigns — much later

Start with a proposed United States campaign set around the 1850s. Begin with walking paths, then introduce horses, wagons, trains and boats through a researched progression. Decide whether rounds represent technological unlocks or the passage of historical years before designing the campaign.

- Use period-appropriate place names, borders, settlements, routes and transport limits, with dates and source notes.
- Create new walking, horse and wagon animations, upgrades and station equivalents.
- Replace modern airports, vehicle cards and discovery facts with era-appropriate content.
- Model terrain, river travel and seasonal constraints at a level suitable for the classroom.
- Review treatment of Indigenous peoples, displacement, slavery and unequal access to transport with age-appropriate historical context.
- Keep simplified game mechanics visibly distinct from documented historical facts.
- Add other time periods only after the first historical campaign is accurate, playable and teacher-tested.

## Supporting work across all campaigns

- Separate simulation rules from world/era content so campaigns can reuse the engine.
- Add seed-based runs and optional daily classroom challenges for fair comparisons.
- Provide difficulty presets, a forgiving practice mode and clear recovery options after losing.
- Track useful learning outcomes without requiring student accounts or personal data.
- Keep map and fact attribution with the content, including licence checks for new artwork and boundaries.
- Test long campaigns, touch devices, fullscreen, save/reload and performance as world size grows.
- Review new cards for usefulness, readable illustrations, unintended combinations and campaign suitability.

Build order: finish UK balance and classroom controls → expand Europe → selectable continents → historical campaigns.
